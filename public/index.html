01 · Our principle

More value from every AI dollar.

Businesses should get more value from every dollar spent on AI. Dsrt AI reduces unnecessary spend, lifts the quality of everyday work, and puts advanced capability within reach of individuals and enterprises alike.

02 · Intelligence at your budget

Designed for multi-agent workflows

Dsrt AI runs many agents in parallel, using every provider you already pay for to verify work until your requirements are met. Follow progress, steer agents, and review results in one place.

Cheaper is only better if it still passes. Dsrt AI pairs every amplified output with a raw baseline, flags unsupported claims and speculative numbers, and reports the amplification overhead alongside the savings.

Recommended entitlements

Designed around the work you do.

The following capabilities describe the proposed plan structure. Launch availability, allowances, and limitations require confirmation.

Work

$9.99 / month · One person

A personal AI workspace for research, writing, analysis, and problem-solving. Use Work Mode to complete a task, then use War Mode to examine how amplification affected an answer, its cost, and response time, subject to availability.

  • Personal workspace: ongoing conversations and searchable history.
  • Work Mode: drafting, analysis, revision, and continued work across messages.
  • LLM Amplification: task understanding, context selection, and supported output checks.
  • Model selection: providers and models included in the plan.
  • Files and voice: supported file types, models, and devices.
  • Personal War Mode: comparisons with a defined monthly allowance.
  • Usage reporting: model use, token consumption, and remaining allowance.

Team

$12 / seat / month · Three-seat minimum

Everything proposed for Work, plus tools to understand who has access, how much the team uses, and which models or workflows provide better results.

  • Member workspaces: individual tasks and conversations.
  • Seat management: invitations, removal, and reassignment.
  • Team roles: owner and administrator access for members, billing, and usage.
  • Central billing: one subscription and invoice for paid seats.
  • Team reporting: usage by member, model, and billing period.
  • Shared War Mode: a shared comparison allowance.
  • Shareable reports: selected comparisons shared without exposing other conversations.
  • Spend alerts and exports: tools for tracking and reconciliation.

At founding pricing, five seats cost $60 / month, ten cost $120 / month, and 25 cost $300 / month, before any separately authorized usage charges.

Enterprise

Custom pricing · Scoped deployment

For companies that need DSRT AI within existing business processes. The enterprise agreement defines systems, access, evaluation, and ongoing support.

  • Organization administration: departments, business units, and designated administrators.
  • Identity integration: company sign-in, provisioning, and offboarding.
  • Provider policies: approved models and credentials by department.
  • Internal integrations: agreed data sources, permissions, actions, and acceptance tests.
  • Cost management: department budgets, allocation, and finance reporting.
  • Workflow evaluation: representative tasks, quality criteria, and cost per successful result.
  • Governance: agreed retention, access records, visibility, and exports.
  • Managed rollout: pilot delivery, training, expansion, escalation, and service targets.

Enterprise deployment

Built around your organization’s requirements.

A company-wide deployment begins with an agreed scope. The enterprise engagement defines how the platform connects to systems, how results are measured, and how the rollout is supported.

01

Deployment design

A written scope identifying departments, workflows, users, approved providers, integration owners, and data requirements.

02

Measured pilot

Representative tasks reviewed for success, quality, latency, and total cost, including amplification overhead.

03

Integration and acceptance

Each agreed integration is tested against a named workflow, source references, and the user’s existing permissions.

04

Department rollout

A schedule for access, expansion approvals, administrator training, and employee support.

05

Ongoing operation

Agreed reporting, support ownership, cost reviews, approved model changes, and expansion processes.

Clear procurement: Enterprise quotes should itemize the recurring platform subscription, any one-time implementation fee, and model usage. Provider charges paid directly through BYOK should be identified separately.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions.

Key information about the proposed plans, pricing, and deployment.

What is DSRT AI?
DSRT AI is an AI workspace and amplification product designed to help individuals and organizations make better use of AI resources.
Which subscription should I choose?
Work is intended for individuals. Team is intended for multiple members with shared administration. Enterprise is for organizations with scoped integration, governance, or rollout requirements.
How much does Work cost?
The supplied Founding Partner price is $9.99 per month for one person. The planned standard price is $19.99 per month. Founding eligibility and duration must be confirmed before purchase.
How much does Team cost?
The supplied Founding Partner price is $12 per seat per month, with a three-seat minimum. Three seats cost $36 per month before any separately authorized usage charges.
Can I purchase one Team seat?
No. The proposed Team subscription starts at three seats. Individuals can consider Work instead.
Are all features in the comparison available now?
Not necessarily. The comparison describes recommended plan entitlements. Launch readiness, supported models, usage limits, and feature availability must be confirmed before purchase.
Is War Mode included?
Personal and shared War Mode allowances are proposed for Work and Team respectively. Availability and the number of included comparisons must be confirmed.
Do I need to bring my own API key?
BYOK is presented as an optional configuration for supported models. Provider availability, credential ownership, and billing arrangements must be confirmed for your plan.
Does DSRT AI guarantee savings?
No universal savings are guaranteed. Results depend on workloads, models, usage, output requirements, and amplification overhead. Quality and total cost should be evaluated independently.
How does Enterprise pricing work?
Enterprise pricing is custom. The agreement should define the platform subscription, implementation fees, model usage, integrations, support, and any direct provider charges.
How long does Founding Partner pricing last?
The duration has not been specified on this page. Confirm the duration, renewal terms, and treatment of seats added later before purchasing.

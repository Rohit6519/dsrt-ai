What you can do

One workspace for the work that sits between a question and a deliverable: investigate, reason, read your files, analyze, write and present.

Ask & reason

Get a direct answer, then push further. Follow-up questions keep the working context so you can test assumptions instead of restarting.

Note — Answers can be wrong. Verify consequential claims against sources.

Research & synthesize

Turn a question into a structured investigation. Compare sources, separate findings from assumptions and keep references beside the work.

Note — Source access depends on connected sources and availability.

Work with documents

Summarize, compare, draft and revise documents with the source material close at hand and citations where supported.

Note — Supported formats and file limits are confirmed at launch.

Analyze tables & data

Interpret tables, identify patterns and explain the calculation with appropriate caveats.

Note — Always check figures against the original dataset.

Write & revise

Create first drafts, edit for clarity and adapt material to different audiences without losing the original meaning.

Note — You remain responsible for the final published text.

Create presentations

Develop narratives, outlines and presentation-ready content from research and notes.

Note — Native slide export is planned and not yet available.

A general-purpose AI assistant and task workspace. You use it to ask questions, investigate topics, work with files, draft and revise deliverables, and complete multi-step tasks in one place.

Coco is a separate engineering product built for coding and repository work. DSRT Chat is the general workspace for research, reasoning, writing and analysis. They share the V4 execution technology but are different products with different workflows.

V4 is the execution and governance layer underneath the products. It is designed to choose and govern how a task runs with attention to cost, quality, latency and verification. Actual outcomes depend on the task, model, provider and measurement methodology.

File upload is part of the intended product. Supported formats, size limits and retention behaviour are confirmed at launch and published on the pricing and documentation pages.

Where sourcing is supported, claims carry references back to the material they came from and you can open the source from the result. References must still be checked before consequential decisions.

Model and provider coverage is confirmed on the pricing and documentation pages. It can change over time and may differ by plan and region.

Usage and cost are shown where the active account and provider support them. The measurement method is documented alongside the figures, because token accounting and provider billing do not always match exactly.

Enterprise administration, data handling and access controls are part of the Enterprise offering and are subject to security and policy confirmation. Specific compliance and deployment statements are provided after review, not inferred from this page.

The founding access programme opens progressively. Anything on this page not yet shipped is labelled Planned, and the current release scope is confirmed at registration rather than assumed from this specification.
